Agri-Food Innovation LMS

Live · real customersFull-stack

A learning and certification platform for the Agri-Food Innovation Academy (AFIA), a training and consultancy business working with food-industry professionals in Nigeria and the UK.

ClientAgri-Food Innovation Academy
RoleFull-stack developer
StackSPA + REST API
StatusLive at agrifiacademy.com

The problem

AFIA trains people in food safety, quality and manufacturing (HACCP, GMP, ISO 9001, BRCGS). Its courses were delivered in person and sold manually. It needed one place where learners in Africa and the UK can find a course, pay for it, learn online and get certified, and where the academy can manage courses, events and content itself.

What I built

  • Course catalogue: 7 faculties and 45 courses at Foundation, Intermediate and Advanced levels, with filtering by faculty and level, and search.
  • Course pages and enrolment: preview pages per course, with pricing in naira and online payment through Paystack.
  • User management: registration, login and a learner area; admin access to manage the catalogue.
  • Content: events (with featured events served from the API), a blog, resources and service pages for the academy's consultancy work.
  • Analytics: product analytics to see which programmes attract interest.

Architecture

  • Front end: a single-page application with client-side routing (courses, faculties, events, blog, login, register).
  • API: a JSON REST API (for example /api/events?featured=true) that the front end calls for catalogue, event and user data.
  • Database: relational models for faculties, courses, levels, enrolments, users, events and posts.
  • Deployment: on a custom domain behind LiteSpeed, with HTTPS.

What it shows

  • Delivering a complete product for a real client, from data model to payments to deployment.
  • Modelling a structured catalogue (faculties → courses → levels) that non-technical staff can grow.
  • Payment integration for an African market.
